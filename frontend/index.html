🌐 Manual IRCTC Website Access

In strict compliance with railway regulations, this application does not launch, control, or interact with external browsers. Please manually open the official IRCTC website:

https://www.irctc.co.in/

Manual Booking Protocol:

  1. Open your regular browser manually and go to the official URL.
  2. Log in 5-10 minutes prior to Tatkal opening (10:00 AM for AC, 11:00 AM for Non-AC).
  3. Have your payment method (eWallet, UPI, NetBanking) ready.
  4. When booking opens, use the "Copy All Details" button here to quickly reference passenger info.
  5. Manually solve the CAPTCHA, verify OTP, and authorize payment.
