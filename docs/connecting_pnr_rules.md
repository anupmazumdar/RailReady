# INDIAN RAILWAYS CONNECTING PNR & BREAK JOURNEY RULES

## 1. Statutory Context: Connecting Journey Feature
When direct trains on a corridor (e.g. New Delhi to Howrah) have no available Tatkal or General berths, passengers can legally book two separate train tickets connecting at an intermediate major railway junction (e.g., New Delhi ➔ Kanpur Central, and Kanpur Central ➔ Howrah).

To protect passengers from train delays, the Ministry of Railways introduced the **Connecting PNR Facility**:
- If Train 1 (Main journey) runs late and arrives after Train 2 (Connecting journey) has departed, the passenger is entitled to a **100% full refund** on the second ticket without any cancellation fee.

---

## 2. Requirements to Qualify for Connecting PNR
1. **Passenger Details Match**:
   - Passenger details (Name, Age, Gender) on both PNRs must be identical.
2. **Station Alignment**:
   - The destination station of the first train and the boarding station of the second train must be identical (or recognized twin stations, e.g., NDLS/DLI/NZM in Delhi, or HWH/SDAH in Kolkata).
3. **Time Buffer Rules**:
   - The scheduled arrival time of Train 1 and the scheduled departure time of Train 2 must have a minimum connection buffer:
     - Minimum buffer at the same station: **1 hour (60 minutes)**.
     - Recommended comfortable buffer: **1.5 to 4 hours**.
4. **Linking on IRCTC**:
   - While booking the second ticket on IRCTC, check the box: **"Connecting Journey Booking"**.
   - Enter the PNR of the first ticket.
   - The system automatically links both PNRs under Indian Railways rules.

---

## 3. Major Transfer Junctions in India
The system incorporates primary transit junctions for split routing:
- **Northern / Eastern Corridor**: Kanpur Central (CNB), Prayagraj Junction (PRYJ), Pt. Deen Dayal Upadhyaya (DDU), Patna Junction (PNBE).
- **Western Corridor**: Kota Junction (KOTA), Vadodara Junction (BRC), Ahmedabad Junction (ADI), Surat (ST).
- **Central / Southern Corridor**: Bhopal Junction (BPL), Itarsi Junction (ET), Nagpur Junction (NGP), Secunderabad (SC), Vijayawada (BZA).
- **Southern Corridor**: Katpadi (KPD), Jolarpettai (JTJ), Erode (ED).
